인간공학 연구실

Department of Railroad Operation Systems Engineering, Korea National University of Transportation

Welcome to the HERO Lab at the Department of Railroad Operation Systems Engineering, Korea National University of Transportation.

한국교통대학교 철도운전시스템공학과 HERO 연구실 방문을 환영합니다.

We study how operators and systems succeed and fail together, to make railway and nuclear operations safer.

HERO 연구실은 인간공학과 인간신뢰도분석(HRA)을 바탕으로 철도와 원자력 운전의 안전을 연구합니다.
