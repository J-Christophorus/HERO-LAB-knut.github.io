Professor 교수

Jooyoung Park, Ph.D. 박주영

Assistant Professor, Department of Railroad Operation Systems Engineering, Korea National University of Transportation

Jooyoung Park works on human reliability analysis (HRA), probabilistic risk assessment and human factors engineering. Before joining KNUT in 2026 he was a staff scientist in the Human Factors and Reliability Department at Idaho National Laboratory (2020–2026), where he worked on dynamic HRA with the EMRALD and HUNTER tools and on simulator-based HRA data collection. His interests include HRA methods and data, human factors and risk assessment for licensing nuclear plants and advanced reactors, automation and autonomous operations, and AI in human factors and risk assessment.

인간신뢰도분석(HRA), 확률론적 위험도평가, 인간공학을 연구합니다. 2026년 한국교통대학교 부임 전 미국 Idaho National Laboratory Human Factors and Reliability 부서에서 Staff Scientist(2020–2026)로 EMRALD·HUNTER 기반 동적 HRA와 시뮬레이터 기반 HRA 데이터 수집을 연구했습니다. HRA 방법론과 데이터, 원전·차세대 원자로 인허가를 위한 인간공학 및 위험도평가, 자동화·자율운전, 인간공학·위험도평가 분야의 AI 활용에 관심을 두고 있습니다.
